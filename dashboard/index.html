How to read IV Expansion + Bucket σ

IV Expansion shows how much IV changed. Bucket σ shows how unusual its log change is for the same stock, time bucket and expiry-tenor group.

Illustration: +18% / +0.6σ is a large change near that stock’s usual pattern; +5% / +3.1σ is a smaller change far above its own historical median.

Both readings use the same observation. σ uses median and 1.4826 × MAD, not the price Z-score. Missing history is unscored; * marks provisional quote quality.
